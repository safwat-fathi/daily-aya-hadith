How can we help?

If you are experiencing issues with the Daily Aya & Hadith Slack bot, have feature requests, or need assistance configuring your workspace, we are here to help!

Common Issues:

  • Bot is not sending daily messages? Ensure it has been invited to the channel (`/invite @Daily Aya and Hadith`).
  • Want to change delivery time? Use the `/settings` or `/subscribe` command in Slack.
